**Privacy Policy**

Vader87 built the Far & Dear app as a free, ad-supported app. This SERVICE is provided by Vader87 at no cost and is intended for use as is.

This page explains what information Far & Dear uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. Far & Dear has no account system and no server of its own.

**Your places stay on your phone**

The places you save (their names, notes and coordinates), your journey log, your unlocked signs and your settings are stored only on your device. I do not receive them.

If Android's automatic backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may back up this data to your Google account and restore it when you reinstall the app or move to a new phone. This backup is managed by Google under your Google account settings, not by me.

**Location**

With your permission, the app uses your device's location while it is open (foreground only) to show the direction, distance and time difference to your saved places, and to recognise when you have arrived at one. Your location is used on the device and is not sent to me. The app never uses location in the background.

**Information sent to other services so the app can work**

* **Weather (MET Norway).** Only when you open "their sky" for a saved place, the app requests that place's weather from the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (api.met.no). The request contains the place's coordinates (rounded to 4 decimal places) and the app name. It does not contain your own location, the place's name or any identifier. You can turn this off in Settings ("Weather on their sky"). [MET Norway terms](https://api.met.no/doc/TermsOfService)
* **Address search and place names.** Searching for an address and showing a place name use your phone's built-in Android geocoder, which may send the search text or coordinates to Google to look them up.
* **Map.** The map you use to place a pin is provided by Google Maps. [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* **Photos.** If you choose a photo for a share card, it is read from your device and combined into an image on your device. It is only sent somewhere if you share it yourself.

**Advertising, analytics and crash reports**

The app shows ads and uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you, such as the Advertising ID and device information:

* [AdMob](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543) — ads. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK, and some US states) you are asked for consent through Google's consent message, and you can change your choice later in Settings ("Privacy choices").
* [Google Analytics for Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/policies/analytics) — anonymous usage events such as "a place was added" or "a sign was unlocked". The app never sends your location, your places' coordinates or their names to analytics.
* [Firebase Crashlytics](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy/) — crash reports.

**Log Data**

If the app crashes, I collect data and information (through Firebase Crashlytics) called Log Data. This may include your device model, operating system version, the configuration of the app, the time and date of the crash and other diagnostic statistics.

**Security**

I value your trust and use commercially acceptable means to protect information. But no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage, is 100% secure, and I cannot guarantee its absolute security.

**Children's Privacy**

Far & Dear is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact me so that I can take the necessary action.

**Deleting your data**

Deleting a place in the app removes it from your device. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device. Data in your Google account backup can be managed in your Google account settings.

**Changes to This Privacy Policy**

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. I will post any changes on this page.

This policy is effective as of 2026-11-17.
